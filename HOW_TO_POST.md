# How to post your comments (about 2 minutes per comment)

Nothing posts automatically. You post every comment yourself, from the dashboard or from Telegram.

## Easiest: the dashboard (laptop or phone)

Open **https://mohammed-rayan07.github.io/comment-crm/** and go to **▶ Post now**. The best card is already open.

1. **Open post on LinkedIn ↗**, then read the post.
2. Pick an option. Change anything you want right in the box (at least the first line, so it sounds like you).
3. **⧉ Copy**, paste it under the post on LinkedIn, and post it.
4. Back on the dashboard, tap **✓ I posted this** under the option you used. If you edited it, your version
   is what the engine learns from. The next card opens.
5. If the card has a connection note: **Open profile ↗**, Connect, Add a note, **⧉ Copy note**, paste, send,
   then tap **🤝 I sent it**.

When someone replies or you start talking in DMs, open **Pipeline**, then drag the card (or click it) to
*They replied*, *In DMs*, *Call booked* or *Client*, and add a note. The **Stats** tab shows how many
comments turn into conversations and calls.

First time on a device, the dashboard asks for a GitHub key. Follow the 4 steps on screen (about 1 minute).

## Or from Telegram

1. **Pick a card.** The pinned message at the top shows how many are waiting. Send `/queue` to see them all,
   best first, with the card number (#18 etc.). Scroll up to that card.
2. **Read the snippet and the 💡 line**, which says why it was picked. Pick the option that sounds most like you.
3. **Tap "🔗 Open post"**. LinkedIn opens on that post.
4. **Copy the comment:** back in Telegram, tap the grey text of your chosen option. It copies.
5. **In LinkedIn:** tap Comment, paste, and **rewrite the first line in your own words** (10 seconds, and it
   makes it sound like you). Then post.
6. **Buyer cards with a 🤝 Connection note:** open the author's profile, tap Connect, then "Add a note",
   paste the note and send.
7. **Back in Telegram:** tap **✅ Posted 1 / 2 / 3** for the option you used.
   If you changed the wording, **reply to the card** (long-press, Reply) with your final comment. That's how
   it learns your voice. ⏭ Skip if the post isn't for you; 🔁 New angles for three fresh drafts.

Taps are applied at the next scheduled run (08:00, 13:00, 18:00 Riyadh). A spinning button is normal,
so don't tap twice.

## Rules that make this work

- **5-8 comments per session, 2-3 sessions a day.** 20 in one sitting from a small account looks automated.
- **Comment soon after new cards land** (just after 08:00, 13:00, 18:00). Early comments get the most reach.
- **Don't use the same stat twice in a day** (e.g. "under 10 seconds", the lead-waste line). Swap in a detail
  from the post itself.
- **Reply to anyone who replies to your comment**, ideally within a few hours. The replies are where
  conversations, and DMs, start.
- **Older cards (2-5 days) are still worth it** on buyer posts: the owner reads every comment on their post.
- Never paste a comment you wouldn't say out loud on a call. Edit or skip.
