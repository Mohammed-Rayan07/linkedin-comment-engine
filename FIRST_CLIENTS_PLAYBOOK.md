## 0. The strategy in 8 lines

1. **One offer, not five services.** The free part is the lead system only: instant reply, follow-up, booking and owner alerts. It's the easiest to measure (reply time and booked appointments before vs after), and you can ship it solo in about 2 weeks.
2. **Websites, reviews, operations and consulting are paid add-ons after the pilot.** If you give everything away free, scope creeps and nothing gets finished.
3. **Free build, client pays their own software, 30-day pilot, one written success metric.** Hit it and they stay at a locked founding rate. Miss it and they keep what was built and walk away.
4. **3 founding spots.** That cap is honest, because you're one person.
5. **What you ask for in return:** a case study with real numbers, a testimonial and 2 referral intros, each asked for *after* results. Never a Google review in exchange for the work (section 2).
6. **Guarantee delivery, never outcomes.** "Live in 14 days or I keep working free until it is." Never promise "2x bookings", because there's no data yet to back it.
7. **Channel partners are the leverage.** One ads agency with 20 brokerage clients is worth more than 20 cold DMs. Bilal and Apoorv already replied.
8. **Open with a free, disclosed after-hours test**, not a pitch. A number about *their* business starts more conversations than anything about you.

## 6. The 20-minute audit call

**Before the call (10 min):** check their ads in the Meta Ad Library, their Google reviews, the site's speed and enquiry form, and your after-hours test result if you ran one.

| Minutes | What to do | Say / ask |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2 | Frame | "20 minutes. I'll ask how leads work today, show you where I think they leak, and if it's a fit I'll explain the pilot. If not, you still get the leak map free." |
| 2-10 | Diagnose (they talk 80%) | "Where do your leads come from, and roughly how many a month?" · "What happens in the first 5 minutes after an enquiry lands?" · "What about at 11 PM, or Friday?" · "How many times does someone follow up before giving up?" · "What's one closed deal or patient worth to you?" · "If you could fix one thing about leads tomorrow, what would it be?" |
| 10-14 | Mirror the cost | Work the formula with their numbers out loud: "So roughly X leads a month get a late reply or none. If even a few of those would have booked, that's about Y a month." Let them correct the numbers; their number beats yours. |
| 14-18 | Offer | The one-paragraph pilot. Then: "We'd agree one number today, for example 90% of enquiries answered within 5 minutes. If we don't hit it, you keep everything." |
| 18-20 | Close the next step | "Want me to send the leak map and the access checklist?" Book the next 15-min slot **before** hanging up. |

### Objections, with honest answers
| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "What's the catch?" | "You're one of my first clients, so I need real results and a case study more than a fee. You pay your own software, which is roughly $50-200 a month." |
| "Have you done this for anyone else?" | "Not for a paying client yet, which is exactly why it's free. Here's what I've built: [MedLog, open code with synthetic data; Advocall; this website]. You'll watch every conversation it has." |
| "We already have a chatbot." | "Most chatbots answer FAQs. Is anyone following up on day 2 and day 5, and booking into the calendar? Can I see what happens after the first reply?" |
| "Leads hate bots." | "Leads hate waiting more. It replies like your team would, hands over to a human the moment someone asks, and your team reviews conversations in week 1." |
| "Will it speak Arabic?" | "Yes, the agent works in Arabic and English. Your team checks the Arabic replies in the first week; I'll tune them with you." |
| "My team will handle it." | "Great. Then let's measure it: I'll send a test enquiry at 11 PM tonight. If someone replies within 5 minutes, you don't need me." |
| "Send me something to read." | "Sure, I'll send the one-page leak map with your numbers. Can we book 15 minutes Thursday to go through it?" Always attach the next step. |
| "Not now." | "Understood. Mind if I check back in 30 days? I'll also send the after-hours test result for free in the meantime." |

## 10. What NOT to do this month

- Don't build more website features. It already passes the trust check.
- Don't offer all 5 services free. Lead system only.
- Don't take a client with fewer than ~50 leads a month or no ad spend. There'll be nothing to measure.
- Don't start building before the one-page agreement and the success metric are agreed in writing.
- Don't discount the founding rate further to close a deal. Free build plus a locked rate is already the no-brainer.
- Don't spend money on ads until there's a case study to put in them.
